      Field mapping
      How these are calculated
      • Done means the status is marked Done in the field mapping. With no Status field, a closed or merged item is done. The done date is the closed or merged date, otherwise when the status last changed.
      • Throughput counts items done per calendar week, weeks starting Monday, over the selected range.
      • Lead time runs from when the issue or pull request was created to its done date. Project fields keep no status history, so a cycle time that starts at In progress cannot be computed from this export.
      • Velocity is points completed per finished iteration, or items when no estimate field is mapped. Plan accuracy is completed divided by committed across finished iterations.
      • Stalled means an item has sat in its current status longer than the p85 lead time, or 14 days when fewer than 5 items have finished.
      • People and summary views combine every loaded project. Each project keeps its own status mapping and estimate field. An item with several assignees counts for each of them. Closed projects are skipped unless you include them.
